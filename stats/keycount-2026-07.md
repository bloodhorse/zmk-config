# keycount — character-frequency ledger, july 2026

The measurement the thumb/base-layer decisions are argued from. Aggregate per-character
counts of what actually *landed* while typing — symbol demand, not physical key seats.

## provenance

- **instrument**: `~/.hammerspoon/keycount.lua` (Hammerspoon `eventtap`, keyDown)
- **window**: 2026-07-11 17:15 → 2026-07-27 23:45 — **16.3 days**
- **volume**: **79,371 presses**, ~4878/day
- **retired**: 2026-07-27, deliberately (`init.lua:49-52`, module commented out, ledger kept)
- **raw ledger**: [`keycount-2026-07.json`](keycount-2026-07.json)

## what it can and cannot tell you

Counts are **post-Karabiner**: they record the character that came out, not the key that
was pressed. `?` here means "the symbol `?` was produced" — under ЙЦУКЕН that was Shift+7,
under US it was Shift+/, and the ledger cannot tell those apart.

Blind spots, by design:

- **no order, no timing** — unordered totals only; sequences and rolls are unreconstructable
- **Cmd/Ctrl chords skipped** — shortcuts are not typing
- **autorepeat skipped** — a held key is one press
- **secure input invisible** — password fields never reach an event tap at all
- **one 16-day slice of July** — seasonal (vacation, less RU correspondence) is not ruled out

Cyrillic letters appear as themselves (`о`, `а`, `т`…), so RU-layout load is directly readable.
Option-key output shows up as the produced glyph (`∂` = Alt+D, `∫` = Alt+B, `∑` = Alt+W …).

genuinely unused, but their *positions* are either terrible (`]`) or occupied by a live
Cyrillic letter (`[` = х). Modifier seats are layout-independent and so always comparable;
symbol seats are not.
